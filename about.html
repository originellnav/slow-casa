About

Slow Casa is a media company dedicated to design-first homes in the Balearic Islands. A directory of houses to rent, and the stories behind their architecture, owners and location. We only include houses we would stay in ourselves, chosen for their design and proxmiy to nature.

History

Slow Casa started in 2026 as a directory of architect-designed houses to rent in the Balearics, built by Swedish/Australian duo, Ida and Luke Jakobson. Inspired by their time living between the Stockholm and the Balearic Islands, they set out to keep track of the most extraordinary houses worth knowing about, and it grew from there.

What became obvious quickly was that the houses were only part of the story. Behind each one was a village that promoted a slower way of living, an architect who designed the home, and a family who had spent years rebuilding something. Those stories became more interesting than discovering the propetirs alone.
